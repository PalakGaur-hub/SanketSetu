02 MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Understanding the sequence.

SanketSetu processes a sequence of hand landmarks over time rather than treating each frame as an isolated image.

MediaPipe extracts 21 hand landmarks from each frame. Their three-dimensional coordinates create 63 features per frame, forming a temporal sequence for the Transformer model.

01 Camera Video input
02 MediaPipe 21 hand landmarks
03 Sequence 154 × 63 features
