ABOUT SANKETSETU

Understanding movement.

SanketSetu is an AI-powered Indian Sign Language gesture recognition system designed to understand hand movements through temporal deep learning.

Instead of looking at one frame at a time, SanketSetu learns from a sequence of hand movements to recognize gestures.

THE IDEA

A gesture is more than a frame.

Sign language is expressed through movement. A hand position can change from one moment to the next, and that sequence carries information.

SanketSetu approaches gesture recognition as a temporal learning problem. The system first extracts hand landmarks from video and then processes those landmarks as a sequence.
